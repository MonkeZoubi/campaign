#nation
**The Freljord** is an unforgiving, frozen wasteland located in the far north of Runeterra. Its people are resilient, born survivors who are deeply tied to the ancient, raw magic of the land. 

The region is currently locked in a bitter, three-way civil conflict:
- **The Avarosan Clan**, led by Ashe, who seeks to unite the tribes through peace and cooperation.
- **The Winter's Claw**, led by Sejuani, who believes only the strong deserve to survive and favors conquest.
- **The Frostguard**, led by Lissandra, who hoard ancient secrets in the deep ice and prepare for a terrifying, forgotten threat from the abyss.
